# Every idea tried on Track 2 (BCI decoding)

Track 2 of the NeurIPS 2026 EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge asks for a 3-class decoder of cued mental commands
(kinesthetic motor imagery, mental calculation, word association) from 4-second EEG windows (41 channels, 120 Hz).
The model sees one window at a time and is told neither the user nor the session.
The score is balanced accuracy, averaged over subject × session × context cells.

The work was done by a team in which a human supervisor steered Claude AI agents: a manager session, a Track 2 session that
ran the experiments, and review sessions. Every experiment was written into a decision log before its numbers
existed (question, arms, pass bar), and its verdict was appended afterwards.
The team finished 13th of 302 with a sealed score of 0.844. (The log's own last board read, at upload time, shows
rank 10: T2-D657.)

This page lists every row of that log. It is a catalogue, not a method paper: most rows are failures, and they are
left as they were recorded.

## The final package and the sealed history

The file that scored 0.844 (W25-ALL6) contains:

- **Three CNN families**, 15 nets each: FBCNet (a filter-bank CNN with a 13-band bank), ShallowNet and IFNet. Each
  family also has a fine-tuned copy per training user. A running spectral fingerprint of the stream picks the
  nearest training user's copy, which is blended with the pooled net at a fixed weight.
- **A foundation-model family** of six ST-EEGFormer-large members, at a share of 0.35 (T2-D345r, T2-D451).
- **A window-mean family**: gradient-boosted trees on per-channel window means, at a share of 0.075 (T2-D516).
- **In-stream adaptation without labels**: at 60, 100 and 150 windows into a stream, small parts are refitted on the
  package's own decoded labels (T2-D511).
- **A decoder per family**: a causal Sinkhorn class-balance step with 10 iterations and a forgetting factor of
  0.95, started from a fixed vector (T2-D285, T2-D304, T2-D459, T2-D476).

## What failed, and what it taught

Each group names the rows, the numbers in them, and the mechanism where a row gives one. The "what it taught"
lines restate the team's own lesson notes; they are readings, not results. The groups overlap with the themed
tables below; the tables are complete, these groups are not.

### 1. Label-free test-time adaptation of the networks

- BatchNorm statistics updated per stream: FBCNet kit test −0.0126, 0/6 seeds up (T2-D100). Later, with BatchNorm
  tracking the stream, every CNN family lost (FBCNet −0.0064, IFNet −0.0151 on test) (T2-D470).
- Entropy or pseudo-label self-training inside predict collapsed to one class: kit val 0.337–0.516 against 0.715
  (T2-D113).
- Masked-reconstruction training of the FM inside predict: 5.4× the runtime, kit test +0.0119 but kit val −0.0230
  (T2-D125). A LayerNorm-only version at a safe step size: mix +0.0003 / −0.0000 (T2-D283).
- Information-maximisation and mean-teacher adaptation of FBCNet: no variant beat no adaptation on the validation
  users (T2-D405, T2-D420). The reading in T2-D420: a new user's confident windows are not the correct ones, so the
  step sharpens the errors.
- In-stream refit of the FM members' main class heads: ten strangers +0.0002, then −0.0014 on fresh seeds
  (T2-D555, T2-D558).
- A per-stream feature centring that read +0.0632 / +0.0418 on a probe head was worth −0.0017 in the package: the
  Sinkhorn decode already absorbs it (T2-D375).

**What it taught.** A gradient step without labels was inert at a safe size and sharpened errors at a larger one.
What paid was refitting small parts on the package's own decoded, class-balanced labels (T2-D511, T2-D553), and
the output-level class balance itself.

### 2. Labelled outside data, and selecting from it

- BNCI2015_004 is the only public corpus with the same three commands. Added to CNN training, FBCNet lost −0.0347
  on ten new participants while the two leaderboard users gained +0.023 (D84). With the later CNN recipe: mix
  −0.0650 / −0.0475 at two doses, 0/12 (T2-D219).
- Supervised pre-training of the CNNs on one outside corpus: typical −0.0044 (3/12). On seven corpora: −0.0106
  (1/12) (T2-D396, T2-D398).
- Supervised pre-fine-tuning of the FM on labelled BCI corpora: kit test −0.0080 alone and −0.0038 in the ensemble
  (T2-D103); on a smaller pool −0.0068 and −0.0107 (T2-D105).
- Selection by performance: the nine outside users were added one at a time, and each one hurt (typical −0.0235 …
  −0.0569, each 0/12). There was no good subset to pick (T2-D453).
- The like-for-like form (same reference, its own head, a low loss weight, its own BatchNorm statistics) removed
  the harm step by step and left nothing that helps: −0.093 … −0.002 (T2-D507).
- Unlabelled outside windows for distillation: mix −0.0241 (0/12) (T2-D294). Outside users' covariances as
  re-colouring targets: mix −0.0112 (0/12) (T2-D295).

**What it taught.** Outside users, even on the same three commands, pulled the pooled networks away from what
transfers to this cohort. The outside data that paid was unlabelled, small and close: the FM's continued
pre-training (D94, T2-D111). A broader pool did not improve on it (T2-D109, T2-D350, T2-FM-D1). More users from the
same setup would still help: the new-user score rises 0.575 → 0.673 from 3 to 9 training users (T2-D410), but all
ten public users were already in training. One exception shipped: an FM member with a first stage on outside
motor-imagery data (T2-D529), after the same idea had missed its bar twice (T2-D455, T2-D488).

### 3. Stronger personalisation

- More copy dose: longer or stronger copy fine-tunes and larger copy weights were flat, or traded returning users
  against new ones (T2-D154, T2-D164, T2-D168, T2-D169, T2-D171, T2-D172, T2-D199, T2-D213, T2-D462, T2-D504).
  Doubling the copy weight cost the ten strangers −0.0196 (T2-D501).
- No statistic told a returning user from a stranger, so the dose could not be gated: fingerprint distance AUC
  0.472 (T2-D317), copy confidence AUC 0.624 (T2-D425), an FM-embedding gate AUC 0.629 / 0.607 (T2-D489), and a
  false-accept rate of at best 0.76 at 95 % detection (T2-D501). Identity itself was solved: routing was
  0.975–1.000 accurate on returning users (T2-D461).
- Per-user parts in the FM: per-user heads on the frozen MOR, mix +0.0004 (T2-D166); per-user LoRA deltas in ST-L,
  seen −0.0134 alone (T2-D189); whole per-user ST-L copies with oracle routing, every arm ≤ 0 (T2-D397).
- In the final build's training composition the fingerprint router had become a hub: all 8 held-out strangers were
  routed to one three-session user's copy (T2-D631). That cost strangers −0.021 at the CNN stage against −0.008 in
  a user-balanced fit (T2-D630). A route space fitted on first sessions only gave 8 strangers +0.009227 at the CNN
  stage (T2-D632) and −0.0008 at the full stack (T2-D638). The final package kept the original router.

**What it taught.** Per-user capacity paid only for small networks at a small fixed blend. Any routing or selection
statistic has to be re-read in the training composition that ships (T2-D635).

### 4. Spatial-invariance augmentation beyond the first dose

- For the CNNs the first dose paid (T2-D195). A wider jitter did not: 15 / 20 mm gave mix +0.0036 / −0.0009 with
  kit test −0.0061 / −0.0111 (T2-D197), and typical −0.0009 on the later stack (T2-D339). A rigid cap shift, a
  frequency warp, a virtual-session drift map and re-colouring variants were null or negative (T2-D295, T2-D229,
  T2-D308, T2-D233, T2-D236, T2-D237). A warp of the FBCNet input by individual alpha frequency lost mix −0.0225
  (T2-D191).
- On the FM members the same family never paid in the package: mix −0.0007 (T2-D198); the best member alone but
  typical +0.0014 in the package (T2-D330); strangers +0.0014 with returning −0.0043, closed by its returning guard
  (T2-D582); a left-right mirror member −0.0018, which the row counts as the fifth augmentation of this family to
  lose in the stack (T2-D618); a surface-Laplacian input view −0.0048 (T2-D480).
- The last upload (W30) was this family again, on five FM members. The lab read strangers +0.0060, returning
  −0.0009 and OLD sealed +0.0024 (T2-D652). The sealed score was 0.838 against 0.844 (T2-D659).

**What it taught.** On the FM this family moved accuracy from returning users to strangers. The member-level
returning reads (−0.0206 / −0.0114) had the sign of the sealed result; the full-chain read on two users did not
(T2-D659). A re-reference is a new view only for a model that cannot learn spatial mixing itself: CAR members
helped the per-channel-token FM (T2-D475) and did nothing for FBCNet (T2-D483).

### 5. Re-tuning weights and decoder constants on the development users

- Weights: an FM weight of 0.4 picked on validation lost kit test −0.0062 on confirmation (T2-D119). A cross-fitted
  re-pick over 12,397 package variants: mix −0.0214 (0/12), with an in-sample optimum of only +0.0017 (T2-D145).

### 13. What the diagnostics said about the ceiling

- Errors are bound by separability, not by signal quality: within a user, signal features predict errors at AUC
  0.43–0.54 against 0.77 for the model's own confidence, and the word class is in 74 % of errors (T2-D409).
- A stranger's failure is missing coverage: the ten-user mean is 0.697 as a stranger and 0.820 once that user's own
  labels are in training (T2-D506).
- The FM members fit their training users completely (training accuracy 1.000 by epoch 40) while held-out
  accuracy plateaus at 0.61–0.68 (T2-D468).
- About 80 % of the M7 step on the sealed set was on the foundation-model side; user-specific changes on the CNN
  side read +0.0080 locally and about +0.001 sealed (T2-D450).
